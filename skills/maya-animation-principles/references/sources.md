# Sources and scope

Frank Thomas and Ollie Johnston's *The Illusion of Life: Disney Animation* is the
framework reference for the twelve principles. See the
[publisher's book page](https://books.disney.com/book/the-illusion-of-life/) and
the [Frank and Ollie principles page](https://frankandollie.com/PhysicalAnimation.html).

Richard Williams' *The Animator's Survival Kit* is the complementary reference
for timing, spacing, and movement study. See the
[publisher's book page](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9780865478978/theanimatorssurvivalkit/).

Walt Disney Animation Studios describes how these ideas serve personality and
performance in its [animation process overview](https://www.disneyanimation.com/process/animation/).

The bundled skill is an original guide to decisions and evidence in Maya. The
MCP workflow, diagnostics, examples, and review procedures are this project's
adaptations. Public author/publisher material was consulted; the complete books
were not supplied or ingested. This is not a chapter-by-chapter digest, a copy of
their demonstrations, or a substitute for studying their illustrations and full
explanations. No scans, quotations, or proprietary exercises are bundled.

Use current task references and the animator's direction to resolve artistic
choices. The books' shared principles inform judgment rather than requiring one
studio style, fixed timing formula, or uniform degree of exaggeration.
